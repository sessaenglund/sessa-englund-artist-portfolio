Sessa Englund About
Sessa Englund is an artist living in Berlin, they hold a BFA from the School of Art + Design at SUNY, Purchase NY (2013).

Englund draws from personal experiences to create sculptural works that explore themes of menace and precariousness. Their work harnesses traditional craftsmanship to investigate the interplay of materialism and the passage of time, embarking on a journey through cultural semiotics to reveal the meanings we attribute to familiarity in artifacts and spaces. Aiming to establish a visceral connection to the human body, Englund incorporates piercing, tattooing, and aging of things, thus exploring the intricate relationship between the human form and inanimate objects. Through the distortion of familiar articles, Englund's sculptures offer uncanny versions that reveal the futility of human effort and the constraints of humanization.

 Englund is currently on a hiatus and not accepting any commissions or work at this time.  

Recent solo and two-person exhibitions include Feigning Death, Hunter Shaw Fine Art (2023); Fool’s Errand, Hunter Shaw Fine Art (2021); Wild Objects with Alicia Adamerovich, Project Pangée, Montreal Canada (2019).

Recent group exhibitions include Dream in Deixis, Tufenkian, Los Angeles (2022); Forever Mall, Shoot the Lobster, Los Angeles (2020); Banquet, in lieu, Los Angeles (2019); and Games of Ceres, King’s Leap, Brooklyn NY (2018).
