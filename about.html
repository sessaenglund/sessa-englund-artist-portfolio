Sessa Englund About
Sessa Englund is an artist working in Berlin, they hold a BFA from the School of Art + Design at SUNY, Purchase NY (2013).

Englund explores themes of materiality, transformation, and the uncanny through sculptural work rooted in craft traditions.

Their practice investigates the relationship between the human body and objects, often distorting familiar forms to reveal tension and fragility.

Recent exhibitions include Feigning Death (2023), Fool’s Errand (2021), Wild Objects (2019).

Group exhibitions include Dream in Deixis (2022), Forever Mall (2020), Banquet (2019), Games of Ceres (2018).
