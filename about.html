Sessa Englund about
Sessa Englund is an artist working in Berlin, they hold a BFA from the School of Art + Design at SUNY, Purchase NY (2013).

Englund draws from personal experiences to create sculptural works that explore themes of menace and precariousness. Their work harnesses traditional craftsmanship to investigate the interplay of materialism and the passage of time, embarking on a journey through cultural semiotics to reveal the meanings we attribute to familiarity in artifacts and spaces.

Aiming to establish a visceral connection to the human body, Englund incorporates piercing, tattooing, and aging of things, thus exploring the intricate relationship between the human form and inanimate objects.

Recent solo and two-person exhibitions include Feigning Death (2023), Fool’s Errand (2021), Wild Objects (2019).

Recent group exhibitions include Dream in Deixis (2022), Forever Mall (2020), Banquet (2019), Games of Ceres (2018).

Press: Art Viewer, New York Times, The New Yorker, ArtNet, Hyperallergic, i-D, ArtNews.

Awards: Urban Glass scholarship (2017), One Minute Archives (2016).

Residencies: Mikro Residency (Palm Springs), East of Elsewhere (Berlin), AADK Murcia, TFNF NYC.

Fairs: Material Art Fair Mexico City (2019).
