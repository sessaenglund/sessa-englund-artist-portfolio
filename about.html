Sessa Englund About
Sessa Englund is an artist living in Berlin, they hold a BFA from the School of Art + Design at SUNY Purchase NY (2013).

Englund draws from personal experiences to create sculptural works exploring materiality, time, and cultural meaning.

Englund is currently on hiatus and not accepting commissions or work.

Recent solo exhibitions include Feigning Death, Fool’s Errand, and Wild Objects.

Group exhibitions include Dream in Deixis, Forever Mall, and Banquet.

Press: New York Times, The New Yorker, Hyperallergic, ArtNews, Interview Magazine.
