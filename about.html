Sessa Englund about
Sessa Englund is an artist working in Berlin, they hold a BFA from the School of Art + Design at SUNY, Purchase NY (2013).

Englund draws from personal experiences to create sculptural works that explore themes of menace and precariousness. Their work investigates materialism, time, and cultural semiotics through crafted objects and altered forms.

Recent solo and two-person exhibitions include Feigning Death (2023), Fool’s Errand (2021), and Wild Objects (2019).

Recent group exhibitions include Dream in Deixis (2022), Forever Mall (2020), Banquet (2019), and Games of Ceres (2018).

Press: Art Viewer, New York Times, The New Yorker, Hyperallergic, ArtNews, Interview Magazine, i-D, and more.

Awards: Urban Glass Merit Scholarship (2017), One Minute Archives (2016).

Residencies: Mikro Residency (USA), East of Elsewhere (Berlin), AADK Murcia (Spain), TFNF NYC.

Fairs: Material Art Fair, Mexico City (2019).
